About Anagen

Anagen helps you analyze your hair and scalp from photos, track changes over time, and get insights regarding hair growth solutions. Analyses are intended for informational purposes only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

Why isn't my analysis working?

  • Check that you have an active internet connection.
  • Make sure the photo is in focus and well-lit.
  • Confirm you've granted the app permission to access your camera and/or photo library in your device settings.
  • Try closing and reopening the app.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions.
  3. Select Anagen to change or cancel your subscription.

For more details, see Apple's guide: How to cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I restore my purchases?

If you've previously purchased a subscription and don't see it active on a new device, open the app's paywall or settings screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
